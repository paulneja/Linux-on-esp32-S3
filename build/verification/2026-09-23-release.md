## What was on the board

Unlike 0.8, these exact bytes are what was flashed and tested: the combined
image above, written at `0x0` with `flash.sh --images`, on the same
ESP32-S3 N16R8 (flash reports manufacturer `0x68`, device `0x4018`, 16 MB)
used for every earlier record.

- **Board suite: 36/36**
  ([`2026-09-23-results.json`](2026-09-23-results.json)), MemAvailable
  3708 kB at the baseline, 3600 kB at the end. 0.8 measured 3744 kB at the
  same point.
- **Beyond the suite: 10/10**
  ([`2026-09-23-extra-tests.json`](2026-09-23-extra-tests.json)), `tainted` 0
  at the end.
- **Factory boots: 20 of 20 clean**, none inconclusive, each rewriting `/etc`
  and `/home` first and reading `dmesg` and the taint flags back
  ([`2026-09-23-soak.json`](2026-09-23-soak.json)). That bounds the fault
  rate at 14% or less at 95%, one-sided; it does not show it is zero.
- **SSH with a PTY from the host over WiFi: pass**, a real `/dev/pts/0`
  ([`2026-09-23-ssh-pty.json`](2026-09-23-ssh-pty.json)).
- **`usb-console`:** off on a factory board with no getty on `ttyGS3`; `on`
  starts one at once and again after a reboot; `off` stops it at once and
  after a reboot. `/proc/consoles` lists `ttyGS3` in every state.
- **WiFi under stress:** 100 `ip link set espsta0 down/up` in a row, then
  25 `wifi connect` in a row. `tainted` 0, no `BUG`, `WARN`, `Oops`, OOM or
  command timeout in `dmesg`, one `udhcpc` throughout, MemAvailable between
  3088 and 3352 kB, and the address back at the end.

Earlier builds of the same fixes passed the same set, the BLE provisioning
dialog was completed once from a Linux host (see BLE.md), and kitty's
`kitten ssh` was checked by hand; those two ran on earlier builds, not these
bytes. Nothing here tests the USB port with a host reading it after
`usb-console on`, or a failing NTP server.
